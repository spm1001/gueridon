## For Claudes to come

The durable knowledge is now in `understanding.md` (Structural lessons) — synthesize from there, not
here. The one insight worth restating: **the ONE-driver invariant forbids a second live *driver*, not
the *removal* of one — so ending a foreign session is safe (subtraction) while attaching to it is not
(addition).** That's why gdn-racuca can SIGTERM any `claude` pid by-pid with no pty (End = graceful,
resumable, removes a driver) but the roster still can't *drive* a session it didn't spawn. And the
roster's `local` bucket is heterogeneous — Claude Desktop-app agents (local-agent-mode cmdline
signature), plain terminal sessions, and RC sessions orphaned by a bridge restart all land there,
identical on the surface; read the `/proc` cmdline, not the label, when you need to tell them apart.
