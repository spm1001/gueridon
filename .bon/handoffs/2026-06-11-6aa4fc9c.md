## For Claudes to come

**"Only getting 200k" in the 1M-context era has multiple independent failure surfaces — diagnose at the real layer, never from a display.** The genuine window is provable two ways: SDK `modelUsage[].contextWindow` (run `claude -p '...' --output-format json`), or proof-by-existence of a >200k-token turn in the transcript (`input_tokens + cache_creation + cache_read`). The display surfaces each lie independently: (1) the **CC `/model` picker** resolves "Fable" to suffix-less `claude-fable-5` = 200k — only the env-pinned `claude-fable-5[1m]` (via `claudefv`) or *typing* `/model claude-fable-5[1m]` gets 1M; (2) **gueridon's gauge** and (3) **bon's session-dashboard hook** both historically divided by a hardcoded 200k; (4) a **stale CC version** (<2.1.170) doesn't know Fable at all. Because transcripts never record the context window, any tool inferring usage% must derive it: `fable`/`[1m]` in the model string → 1M, and input already >200k → 1M regardless of model string (opus[1m] logs as plain `claude-opus-4-8`). The classifier-downgrade→picker-reselect path is the most likely way a user lands on a degraded 200k Fable mid-session; the negative-context-use symptom is the tell.
