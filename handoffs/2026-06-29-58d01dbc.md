## For Claudes to come

**Guéridon observes CC from outside via structured substrate, never the TUI.** Three primitives the
launcher now rests on, each a structured signal rather than view-scraping:
1. **SIGTERM = a faithful `/exit`** for a `claude` process — triggers its `process.on("SIGTERM")`
   GracefulShutdown (SessionEnd hooks fire, JSONL flushes, session stays `--resume`-able). NOT SIGHUP
   (survived), NOT typing `/exit` into the pty (swallowed by menus / mid-turn).
2. **`stop_reason: end_turn` on the latest MAIN-thread assistant message** (filter `parent_tool_use_id`
   null so a finished subagent can't false-signal) = "turn done / ready for input". Readable from the
   session JSONL tail; the bridge finds the JSONL from just the folder (newest `*.jsonl` with
   `mtime ≥ spawnedAt`). Caveat: `/open` latency is UNBOUNDED — always pair with a timeout.
3. **`/proc` `comm=="claude"`** = discover every live session, even ones Guéridon didn't spawn (it has no
   pty handle to *drive* those — discovery ≠ control). This is how the roster sees terminal sessions.

The throughline: when you need to know what a CC session is doing without driving it, read the substrate
(hook logs, JSONL `stop_reason`, `/proc`), never scrape the rendered view. View-sniffing is fragile;
structured signals are reliable and testable.
