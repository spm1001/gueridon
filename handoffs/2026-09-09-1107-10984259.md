## For Claudes to come

**The `/resume` picker is a filtered view, not a listing.** Claude Code's session lister drops SDK-spawned transcripts (`entrypoint` in `sdk-cli`, `sdk-ts`, `sdk-py`) unless the current process is itself an SDK entrypoint, and drops sidechains, daemon kinds, team-name mismatches, superseded sessions and bookkeeping-only files. Every phone, remote-control, `-p`, Guéridon and email-loop session is therefore invisible there by design, and its search box matches titles only. Consequences: an "our session isn't in /resume" report is not a bug to chase; the uuid route (`teleport-id.sh`) or a lister of our own (`remote-sessions`, Guéridon's session index) is the product; and a `--fork-session` resume converts a hidden session into a visible one because the fork is written by an interactive process. Measured 2026-09-09 on CC 2.1.266, bundle read plus live picker under hublot on both seats.

**The session registry is the join the estate kept re-deriving, and it is ephemeral.** `~/.claude/sessions/<pid>.json` (and the commis seat's directory) pairs the teleport id (`bridgeSessionId`), the local uuid, cwd, entrypoint, tmux pane and, by which directory it sits in, the wallet — and is deleted seconds after the process exits. Anything that needs those fields after death has to journal them while the record exists; that is gdn-daluto. And a live record means a live driver: never plain-`--resume` a uuid that a registry record still names — while the converse does not hold: no record does not mean no driver will come (next paragraph).

**Ending a bridge child does not end a phone conversation.** The remote-control server respawns a child on the same transcript the next time a message arrives from the app. A phone session that has hit its model limit therefore stays writable-from-the-phone indefinitely, and the only safe continuation on another wallet is a fork. Archive the conversation in the app if the original must stay quiet.

**A guard that asks a human to do a mechanical step will be a red light nobody reads.** The version ratchet's purpose was "never ship changed content under an unchanged version"; its mechanism was "email a human to bump". Seven consecutive red days and no action. The repair kept the purpose and automated the mechanism, and the losing reading (hand-written changelog lines, the four-day wedge that made quarantine softer than abort) is recorded beside the change. When a CI check goes red for the same reason day after day, ask whether the check's mechanism can do the thing it is asking for.
