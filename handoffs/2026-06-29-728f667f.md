## For Claudes to come

`claude --remote-control [name] "<initial prompt>"` is a **launch flag** (CC v2.1.195) that starts an *interactive, remote-control-active* session — it prints a `https://claude.ai/code/session_<id>` URL, accepts an initial prompt it runs autonomously, stays fully tool-capable, and works spawned **headlessly** (in a pty/tmux, non-Vertex/Teams). That is the mechanism for *launching a remote Claude session from mobile* — the gap Anthropic's stack otherwise leaves open (`/rc` itself is attach-only to interactive TTY sessions; there is no remote-*launch*). It reshapes Guéridon's future from "hand-rolled mobile client" to "thin launcher that hands the conversation to claude.ai's native UI," collapsing the SSE/state-builder/render back-half. Folder trust for a fresh launch lives in `~/.claude.json` → `projects["<abs-path>"].hasTrustDialogAccepted` (a *shared* file — pre-seed atomically). Full framing + the 5-outcome plan are in `understanding.md`.
