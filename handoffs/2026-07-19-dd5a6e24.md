The durable knowledge is now in `understanding.md` (2026-07-19 entry) and
gdn-cepalu's `--how` — synthesize from there. The one reusable trick worth
restating: **to salvage a live bridge session before a restart**, recover its
session UUID by matching the process start-time (`ps -o lstart`, local TZ) to each
candidate `~/.claude/projects/<enc>/<uuid>.jsonl`'s first-entry timestamp (UTC —
mind the offset), because RC sessions carry no `--session-id` in their cmdline and
don't hold the JSONL fd open between turns. Then `claude --resume <uuid>` in a
**login** shell (runs under `user@.service`, so it survives the gueridon cgroup's
`KillMode=control-group` restart), ending the bridge's copy first to keep one
driver per session. gueridon itself is now on **node 22** (NodeSource static build);
the load-bearing detail is the N-API-rebuilt `pty.node` (no `libnode.so.115`) — the
